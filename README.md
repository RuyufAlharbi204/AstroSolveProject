# AstroSolve
### Conjunction Risk Prediction in Low Earth Orbit (LEO)

## Overview

AstroSolve is an ongoing research project focused on developing a machine learning framework to predict satellite conjunction risks in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). Using historical Conjunction Data Messages (CDMs), the project aims to forecast potential collision events, identify high-risk orbital regions, and visualize risk patterns through interactive heatmaps.

**Research Status:** Under development. Detailed project information is not publicly available at this stage.

## Abstract

This research proposes a predictive framework for reducing satellite collision risks in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). Historical Conjunction Data Messages (CDMs) will be used to train machine learning models to forecast potential conjunction events.

The predicted risks will be analyzed to identify spatial and temporal orbital hotspots where collision risks may be elevated. These patterns will be visualized through interactive heatmaps to support risk assessment, satellite operations, and space traffic management.

The framework integrates computer science, artificial intelligence, and electrical engineering, combining data preprocessing, predictive modeling, orbital mechanics, and visualization into a unified approach to collision risk reduction.
