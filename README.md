## Project AstroSolve
Conjunction Risk Prediction in Low Earth Orbit (LEO)
This repository contains the ongoing research and development of a predictive framework aimed at reducing satellite collision risks in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). The project leverages historical Conjunction Data Messages (CDMs) and machine learning to forecast potential conjunction events, identify high-risk orbital "hot-zones," and visualize them via interactive heatmaps.

Note: This research is currently under development. Full project details are not publicly available at this time.

## Abstract
This research proposes a predictive modeling framework aimed at reducing conjunction risks in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). Historical Conjunction Data Messages (CDMs) will be used to train machine learning models to efficiently forecast potential collision events. The predictive outputs will then be analyzed to identify high-risk regions spatially and temporally, also known as “hot-zones,” within LEO. These highly congested regions of the orbit have a high probability of collision between objects. The results will be visualized through interactive heatmaps to support decision-making and policy development. The framework integrates expertise across computer science, artificial intelligence, and electrical engineering, combining data preprocessing, algorithmic modeling, orbital mechanics, and visualization into a cohesive risk-reduction tool for space operators.
